About

Stories in rhythm —
breath, body,
culture.

Ian Gwagi H is a Nairobi-based poet, musician, essayist, and food writer working at the intersection of oral tradition and contemporary form. His practice spans page poetry, spoken word, original composition, and long-form cultural criticism — with a running obsession with what food reveals about power, memory, and belonging.

His work has appeared in literary journals, on stages from Nairobi to Kampala, and in field notes from agroecology projects across East Africa.
